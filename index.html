🙋 投票：错在哪里？(A) 工时少算 (B) 加班费错了 (C) 没问题🙋 Vote: Where's the error? (A) Hours wrong (B) Overtime rate wrong (C) Looks fine
✗ 加班费错了 — 应付时薪 1.5 倍Overtime rate is wrong — should be 1.5×
加州法律：每天超 8 小时或每周超 40 小时 → 时薪 × 1.5 ($24)。CA law: over 8 hrs/day or 40 hrs/week → rate × 1.5 ($24).
正确金额：10 小时 × $24 = $240（少给了 $80）。每天超 12 小时则要 × 2 ($32)。Correct: 10 hrs × $24 = $240 (short $80). Over 12 hrs/day = 2× rate ($32).
税号 · ITIN vs SSNTax IDs

ITIN 与 SSN 的区别 ITIN vs Social Security Number

不论身份，您都可以报税 — 用 ITIN 就行 No matter your status, you can file taxes — use ITIN

SSN

社会安全号码 · 9 位Social Security Number · 9 digits
  • 合法居民、公民、绿卡持有人Citizens, green-card holders, work-permit holders
  • 在 Social Security Administration 申请Apply at the Social Security Administration
  • 免费 · 当面办理Free · In-person at SSA office
  • 可申请政府福利Qualifies for government benefits
  • 退休时可领社安金Earns Social Security retirement credits

ITIN

个人税号 · 9 位（9 开头）Individual Taxpayer ID · 9 digits (starts with 9)
  • 没有 SSN 的人也可申请（不论身份）For anyone without an SSN — regardless of status
  • 向国税局 IRS 申请（表 W-7）Apply to IRS using Form W-7
  • 免费 · 可在 CSC 办理Free · CSC can help process
  • 可报税 · 申请儿童税收抵免 (CTC)Can file taxes · claim Child Tax Credit
  • 不能用于工作授权Does NOT grant work authorization
📋 重要：报税不会被举报给移民局 — 国税局法律规定保密。📋 Important: Filing taxes does NOT report you to immigration — IRS confidentiality is protected by law.
用 ITIN 报税可建立报税记录 — 将来办移民身份时有帮助。Filing with ITIN builds a tax record — helpful for future immigration cases.
